# KBAI weekly notes: source and correction record

This is a historical correction record. The personal course and its authoring
source are no longer part of the public app or current repository tree.
Previously installed copies remain private browser data; compatibility tests
use the already-published Git revision. New users import their own generated
course rather than receiving KBAI automatically.

All 26 lessons are summarized in the 17 actual Fall 2026 weeks. Revision 6
replaces the default reading narrative, not the course identity or private data.
The ebook is the content authority; the current syllabus is the administrative
authority. PDFs and extracted transcripts are not distributed.

## Source review

The supplied **357-page 2016 KBAI ebook** was read locally by column. Printed
figures were inspected where transcript fragments omitted important content or
corrupted terminology: block configurations, the pitcher impasse, arch learning,
robot clauses, line/junction constraints, the error-detection algorithm, advanced
representations, and the figure-only second principle in the wrap-up.

Plain-language notes headings are authored headings, not claimed ebook anchors.
Each substantive section cites the actual source heading(s) and PDF viewer
pages for its discussion. Original schematics cite both that discussion and
their original authorship. The source's mangled transcript words are not copied
as technical vocabulary.

## Instructional correction

The representative Week 3 was reviewed against the source before extending the
approach. A further independent review covered Logic, Version Spaces and
Constraint Propagation, including printed proof clauses and actual meal records.
The reviewer requested concrete implication and model-change tables rather than
using an abstract graph as a substitute for seeing the inference.

Removed material includes repeated Preview/Keep-your-next-step scaffolding,
fragmented exercise steps, deployment detours, generic engineering transfer
assignments, and later-lesson foundation filler. Narrative follows the source's
teaching order. Practice remains secondary, not the explanation itself.

Tests no longer reward 1,500-word floors, multiples of baseline length, five-node
graphs or four animation steps. They retain contract, privacy, mapping, rights,
source-bound and historical-question guards and add section order, real locators,
source-specific distinctions, concrete table values and diagram readability.
Those guards catch regressions; they do **not** certify instructional truth.

## Update and persistence

Stable IDs remain `kbai` and `lesson-01` through `lesson-26`. Current questions,
all three question-bank snapshots, and recall cards retain their prior IDs and
learner-facing content. Source/asset declarations and fingerprints are unchanged.

Rewritten explanations have new `lesson-XX-notes-*` block IDs. The old focus map
is retained as a historical map, not rewritten to pretend old quotations refer
to new text. Installing revision 6 uses the normal update preview and explicit
confirmation. Removed quotes are preserved as orphaned; changed quotes are
flagged; removed Focus anchors require deliberate selection; changed or removed
diagram positions are not guessed. Notes, highlights, bookmarks, checks,
question histories, active snapshots, source attachments and the old private
archive remain separate and preserved.

Existing courses are **not** replaced on startup. Custom and imported course
data remain untouched until the user confirms a replacement through local JSON
import. Fresh installations now start with an empty personal library.

## Remaining limitations

Optional external reading texts, authenticated live announcements, video
timestamps and missing lecture material remain **unverified**. These notes
summarize the supplied ebook; they do not reproduce every textbook exercise,
claim a complete modern research survey, or supply assessed-work solutions.
The Logic lesson includes the book's quantified language and propositional
refutations, not a new full first-order resolution course.

The supplied syllabus's end-of-course-survey date discrepancy remains explicitly
flagged. No deadline was inferred from the older ebook or silently reconciled.
Private browser data remain origin-bound; this correction does not migrate data
between the former host and the shared lab domain.
